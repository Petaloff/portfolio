The hero scene is a single `InstancedMesh`. Every section of the page defines a
target formation, and each frame the cubes ease towards it.

## Formations

1. Text sampled from a 2D canvas
2. A stack of card-shaped slabs
3. A grid, one cube per tool in the stack
4. Scattered debris near the contact section
